# Method Decisions

Decisions made after Phase 0 (2026-11-10). Jake approved the Phase 0 memo's four recommendations and asked for the project to be finished, so these were applied without a further stop. Each one is reversible by editing the script named.

| # | Decision | What was chosen | Why | Script |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Excess taxi measure | Primary: FAA ASPM taxi-out delay against FAA's unimpeded time, given to each BTS flight from its **actual pushback hour**. Low end of range: BTS taxi-out minus the carrier's quiet-hour median, floored at zero | FAA's unimpeded time is the official reference. It is published per airport and hour only, so per-carrier views use the BTS baseline. Pushback hour is used because a late flight taxis in a later, often busier hour | `04_build_flights.R`, `12_taxi_out.R` |
| 2 | Raw cache | `C:\DataCache\PHX_Airport_Ops` on JAHE, set in a local `.Renviron` as `PHX_CACHE_DIR` | Keeps about 1.3 GB of national files off OneDrive | `00_setup.R` |
| 3 | FAA data | OPSNET daily (2024-2025) and ASPM hourly (17,440 hours) saved from the FAA report tables in Chrome; files committed with their query settings in the header | The FAA site serves reports to browser sessions only | `02_clean_faa.R` |
| 4 | Inherited delay | PLAN.md definition: min(inbound lateness, departure delay). Sensitivity: subtract each turn's schedule slack above the carrier's 10th-percentile turn | The plain definition is easy to explain; the slack version shows how much schedule padding absorbs | `10_inherited_delay.R` |
| 5 | Stages ranked | Three non-overlapping stages per BTS departure: inherited gate delay, PHX-originated gate delay, excess taxi-out. FAA OPSNET delays are shown beside the ranking as an airfield cause overlay | OPSNET delays sit inside gate and taxi time already (a ground stop holds aircraft at the gate). Adding them would count the same minutes twice | `20_stage_ranking.R` |
| 6 | Dollar conversion | Gate minutes: A4A 2025 crew + aircraft ownership + other ($50.72/min), with full block cost ($98.41/min) as the high case. Taxi minutes: full block cost. Passenger time: FAA/DOT all-purpose air value $47.10/hour in 2015 dollars, raised to 2025 dollars with CPI-U, range $38.90 to $58.30. Passengers per departure from FAA Table 3-6 seats times load factor (mainline vs regional) | Block time starts at pushback, so a minute held at the gate burns little fuel. All inputs are in `data/reference/cost_inputs.csv` with sources and must be verified by Jake | `20_stage_ranking.R` |
| 7 | "Degrades first" (Act 2) | **Primary: the stage whose annual delay minutes rise the most at +15% traffic.** Second view: for each stage, the growth level at which its busiest hour's average per departure crosses 15 minutes; stages already above 15 minutes today are reported as "already over" with their increase | Evening PHX-originated gate delay already averages more than 15 minutes per departure at 0% growth, so the plan's threshold test cannot rank it. Annual minutes is defined for every stage and converts to dollars | `30_growth_scenarios.R` |
| 8 | Load response | Fitted within the same clock hour across days (hour, day-of-week and month fixed effects, standard errors clustered by date). Inherited delay is held constant under growth | Comparing different hours would mix load with the build-up of delay through the day. Inherited delay comes from other airports' networks | `30_growth_scenarios.R` |
| 9 | Weather effects | Regression with hour, day-of-week, month and carrier fixed effects, clustered by date | Heat falls in the afternoon when delay is already high; the controls separate the two | `14_weather.R` |
| 10 | Processed format | `.rds` instead of Parquet | Avoids an extra large package; the tables are only read by R | all |
| 11 | "Best return" | The stage with the most addressable dollars per year. Addressable = minutes above a measured benchmark at PHX (best carrier in the same hour for gate delay; the quiet-hour level for taxi delay). The cost of any fix is not in public data, so "return" means recoverable value, stated at 25%, 50% and 100% capture | No public source prices the fixes; the report says so plainly | `20_stage_ranking.R` |
